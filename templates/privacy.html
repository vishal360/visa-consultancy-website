Privacy notice

How {{ brand_name }} collects, uses and protects your personal data.

What we collect

When you book a consultation or send an enquiry we collect your name, email address, phone number, nationality, the service you selected, your chosen appointment slot and anything you write in the message field. We also record the IP address the submission came from, purely to detect abuse of the booking form.

Why we collect it

We use your details solely to arrange and deliver the consultation you requested, to contact you about your appointment, and to advise on your visa application. We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties beyond the authorities directly handling your application.

How long we keep it

Booking and enquiry records are retained for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet our professional record-keeping obligations, after which they are deleted. You may request earlier deletion at any time.

Where it is stored

Submissions are stored in our own database on infrastructure we control, and notification emails are delivered through our mail provider. Access to the consultant dashboard is restricted to authenticated staff accounts.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, to have inaccurate details corrected, to withdraw your consent, and to request erasure of your record. To exercise any of these, email {{ contact_email }} and we will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

The public website sets no tracking or advertising cookies. Your theme preference is saved in your browser's local storage, which never leaves your device. A single session cookie is used only on the staff dashboard, to keep consultants signed in.

Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to {{ contact_email }}, by phone on {{ contact_phone }}, or by post to {{ office_address }}.
